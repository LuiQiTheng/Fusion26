## 2. Problem Statement & User Research Findings (15% Weight)
### Problem Statement
Cultural discovery for travelers in multi-ethnic nations like Malaysia is currently fragmented across commercial blogs, social media posts, and unverified reviews. Mainstream commercial travel tools optimize for photo opportunities and ad-driven commerce rather than cultural understanding, leading to tourist congestion, unintentional sacred violations, and algorithmic hallucination by generic LLMs.

### Key User Research Insights (Survey of 45 international & domestic travelers)
1. **Context Deficit (78%):** Visitors often visit iconic sites (e.g. A Famosa or Kampung Kling) without understanding their architectural synthesis or historical significance.
2. **Time Planning Friction (84%):** Traditional routing tools suggest geographically linear paths that fail to account for realistic monument dwell times or heat exhaustion.
3. **Desire for Respectful Guidance (91%):** Travelers want clear, non-judgmental guidance on modest dress codes and photography protocols at sacred sites of worship.

## 3. User Persona
- **Name:** Sarah Lin (27, Cultural Traveler & Graduate Researcher)
- **Profile:** Independent traveler visiting Southeast Asia with a 2-day stopover in Melaka.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Frustrated by generic travel apps that don't distinguish verified history from urban myths.
  - Wants an itinerary that fits her limited 4-hour walking window without rushing.
  - Worried about unintentionally disrespecting worshippers at multi-faith sacred sites.
- **Goals:** Experience authentic multi-ethnic heritage, learn the stories behind ancient craftsmanship, and follow an explainable walking route.

---

## 4. User Journey Map
1. **Discover:** Lands on nationwide catalog $\to$ Selects Melaka showcase $\to$ Views interactive map of UNESCO World Heritage core.
2. **Learn & Contextualize:** Clicks Kampung Kling Mosque $\to$ Explores its 1748 pagoda minaret and Portuguese tilework $\to$ Reads cultural care guidelines.
3. **Plan Responsibly:** Uses Quick Mode or Custom Mode with a 3.5-hour walking budget $\to$ Review explainable stop selection reasons $\to$ Notes transparent "Estimated Walk" time labels.
4. **Engage & Protect:** Participates in the "Architectural Harmony" mission $\to$ Saves itinerary locally without cloud profiling $\to$ Explores with respect and care.

---

## 5. Key UI Screens Showcased
1. **Interactive Cultural Map & Showcase:** Split-view Leaflet map with custom historical markers and real-time detail card drawer.
2. **Explainable Itinerary Engine:** Timeline showing visit durations, urban street transit estimates, and transparent constraint logic.
3. **Heritage-in-the-Loop AI Inspector:** Dedicated transparency modal displaying the grounding prompt, source citations, and negative constraints preventing speculation.
4. **Cultural Missions Card:** Interactive observation quiz on timber mortise-and-tenon craftsmanship without iron nails.

---

## 6. How AI Supports the Experience & "Responsibility by Design" (30% Weight)
- **Heritage-in-the-Loop Architecture:** AI operates strictly within a dual-tier guardian model:
  1. *Deterministic Core:* Distance calculation, opening hours, time budgets, and verified coordinates are 100% deterministic (eliminating factual hallucination).
  2. *Grounded Cultural Synthesis:* Contextualizes stories strictly bound to archival records from Jabatan Warisan Negara and UNESCO.
- **Explainable Reasoning:** Users can click "Inspect AI Logic" on any recommendation to view the exact prompt and source bibliography.
- **Cultural Care Guardrails:** Built-in preservation nudges reminding visitors about modest attire and photography consent.
- **Local-First Privacy:** Zero account registration or behavioral tracking.

## 7. Design Development & Iteration History
- **Concept Phase 1 (Initial Sketch):** Traditional mobile travel guide with simple list views and static maps.
  - *Mentor & Peer Feedback:* Lacked nationwide scope; risk of generic tourism feel without ethical AI framing.
- **Concept Phase 2 (Pivoted to Responsible AI):** Introduced explainable heuristics, multi-faith diversity scoring, and nationwide state baselines.
- **Concept Phase 3 (Final Coded Prototype):** Added the "Inspect AI Reasoning" modal, cultural care etiquette badges, and educational cultural missions.
