# FUSION 2026 Student Design Challenge — Concept Video & Demo Script

> **Deliverable 2 Requirements:**
> - **Duration:** 2:30 – 2:50 minutes (Hard limit: max 3:00 minutes)
> - **Resolution:** 1080p MP4
> - **Mandatory Inclusions:** (1) Problem scenario, (2) Prototype walkthrough, (3) One responsible design decision.

---

## Storyboard & Timing Breakdown

### Scene 1: The Problem Scenario (0:00 – 0:35)
- **Visual:** Split screen: A traveller overwhelmed by disjointed travel blogs, commercial booking ads, and unverified opening hours. Cut to a delicate heritage facade crowded with tourist queues.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "Every year, millions of travellers visit Malaysia seeking its vibrant multi-ethnic culture. Meet Sarah, an international visitor with only half a day in Melaka.
  > She faces two huge frustrations: online guides are fragmented and treat sacred traditions as photo backdrops, while generic AI tools hallucinate schedules and suggest impossible, exhausting itineraries that ignore cultural etiquette.
  > How can we design an experience that makes heritage accessible, without sacrificing cultural respect and accuracy?"

---

### Scene 2: Solution Overview & Nationwide Baseline (0:35 – 1:05)
- **Visual:** Open Malaysia Heritage Explorer homepage. Show sleek hero banner and scroll down to the 16 states & federal territories overview grid. Click into Penang and Terengganu to demonstrate national baseline.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "Introducing Malaysia Heritage Explorer: a responsible digital platform connecting authentic heritage storytelling with explainable trip planning.
  > Unlike typical travel apps that only focus on tourist hotspots, we provide a structured cultural baseline across all 13 states and 3 federal territories, verified against national archives."

---

### Scene 3: Melaka Showcase & Interactive Mapping (1:05 – 1:40)
- **Visual:** Click into "Melaka Showcase". Show the split-screen view: Leaflet interactive map with custom pins on the left, and curated place cards on the right. Click "Kampung Kling Mosque". Open the story modal.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "In our Melaka showcase, visitors uncover 500 years of living history.
  > Clicking Kampung Kling Mosque reveals its 1748 architectural synthesis: a pagoda-like minaret, European glazed tiles, and Hindu-influenced timber beams.
  > Every historical claim links directly to official registers from Jabatan Warisan Negara and UNESCO."

---

### Scene 4: Smart Constraint-Based Planning (1:40 – 2:10)
- **Visual:** Navigate to "Trip Planner". Select Quick Mode (3.5 hours, walking). Click "Generate Itinerary". Show the timeline with sequential stops, distance estimates, and selection reasons. Click "Remove Stop" to show dynamic recalculation.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "Next, Sarah plans her visit. Rather than unconstrained generative guessing, our smart planner uses deterministic constraints.
  > It calculates realistic walking speeds (4.5 km/h), physical visit durations, and cultural diversity bonuses so users don't encounter a monocultural journey.
  > Notice how travel segments are transparently labelled as 'Estimated Walk' or 'Simulated Transit'—we never fake live telemetry."

---

### Scene 5: The Responsible Design Decision (2:10 – 2:40)
- **Visual:** Click the "Inspect AI Logic" button on the itinerary or place card. The Responsible AI Grounding Drawer opens, revealing the exact system prompt, cited archival sources, and cultural care guardrails. Cut to the "Cultural Missions" tab and complete a quiz with confetti.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "Here is our core responsible design decision: Heritage in the Loop.
  > Through our 'Inspect AI Reasoning' drawer, judges and visitors can audit the exact prompts, archival citations, and cultural care guardrails applied.
  > At sacred temples and mosques, our system automatically surfaces modest dress codes and photography boundaries to prevent cultural harm.
  > Meanwhile, our educational missions gamify respectful observation—learning how the Melaka Sultanate Palace was built without nails."

---

### Scene 6: Closing & Call to Action (2:40 – 2:55)
- **Visual:** Show saved trip page persisting data in browser storage without requiring an account. Display SDG 11.4 badge and team credits.
- **Narrator Voiceover:**
  > "Malaysia Heritage Explorer proves that AI in tourism should not invent stories—it should remember, care, and protect living culture.
  > Built for FUSION 2026. Thank you."

---

## Recording Checklist
- [ ] Screen recording tool set to 1080p, 60fps (OBS Studio recommended).
- [ ] Chrome browser zoom at 100% or 110% for crisp typography.
- [ ] Voiceover recorded in a quiet room with no background hum.
- [ ] Royalty-free background music (low volume, warm acoustic/gamelan-inspired ambient track).
- [ ] Total video duration confirmed between 2:30 and 2:50.
